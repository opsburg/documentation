# The atelier

The atelier is the tailor's island beside the bridge between the city gates and the city. There you dress your citizen, that is the figure that stands for your user account in Opsburg. The look is cosmetic: it does not change what you can do in a city.

## When you see it

- **Right after signing up.** A new account goes to the atelier before the city. The board reads **Dress your citizen** and starts from a look drawn at random from your username, so a reload shows the same draft. Choose **This is me** to save it and fly on to the city.
- **Any time later.** Choose **Your look** on the city board (or on the founding board) to come back. The board then reads **Your look**: choose **Keep this look** to save, or **Back to the city** to leave without saving.

Your citizen stands on the turntable in front of a tall mirror. Drag the turntable to turn the citizen around; let go and it turns back to face you. The citizen hops each time its look changes.

## What you can change

Use the arrows beside each part to cycle through its options.

| Part | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Build** | Slim, Sturdy, Broad |
| **Hair** | Short, Long, Bun, Curly, Mohawk, Bald |
| **Face** | Clean shaven, Mustache, Beard, Mutton chops |
| **Hat** | Bare head, Top hat, Bowler, Flat cap, Aviator cap |
| **Eyes** | Nothing, Goggles, Monocle, Spectacles |
| **Outfit** | Waistcoat, Long coat, Overalls |
| **Fittings** | Brass, Copper, Steel (the metal details of the outfit) |
| **Tool** | Wrench, Lantern, Spyglass, Empty hands |

The tool also names your trade, shown under the parts:

| Tool | Trade |
| --- | --- |
| Wrench | Mechanic: keeps the machines running. |
| Lantern | Lamplighter: walks the night shift when the pager rings. |
| Spyglass | Lookout: watches every gauge and every log. |
| Empty hands | Free hands, still choosing a trade. |

Three colors complete the look:

- **Skin**: one of eight tones.
- **Hair**: one of eight colors, or any color from the color picker.
- **Cloth**: the color of the outfit, one of eight colors or any color from the color picker.

**Surprise me** draws a whole new look at random. Nothing is saved until you choose **This is me** or **Keep this look**; the whole look is saved at once.

## Where your citizen appears

Once saved, your citizen stands on a small brass landing in front of the city, with your username above it.

Without WebGL 2 the console shows the same atelier board as a flat panel, without the 3D citizen to look at.
